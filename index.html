HOW TO PLAY

Chaotic shopping cart brawler — ram rivals off the arena, stack bonuses, and end the round with the most points. Ties go to Sudden Death.

◇ HOW TO SCORE

You only score when you force a rival off — self-falls count for nothing. Bonuses add first, then combo multipliers apply.

  • Edge KO — 1 pt, off the perimeter
  • Hazard KO — 2 pts, into the center pit or corner voids
  • Critical — +1 when your ram is high-speed
  • Leader down — +1 for KOing the sole leader (they glow)
  • High ground — +1 on Sundial from the podium

◇ COMBOS

Chain KOs before the ~5s streak fades — each tier multiplies the whole reward: RAMPAGE ×1.5 · SAVAGE ×2.0 · CARNAGE ×3.0

◇ LIVING STORE

  • Cargo bay — scoring fills your cart; fuller = heavier handling, bigger spill
  • Spill rush — forcing a spill grants a short speed comeback buff
  • PA directives — mid-round rule twists (~18s); watch the HUD chip under the timer:
FLASH SALE Harder rams
DOUBLE BAG KO rewards ×2
EXPRESS LANE Faster boost charge
SPILL BONUS +1 per forced spill
RUSH HOUR Everyone drives faster
