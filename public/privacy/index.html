Data and privacy details

This notice covers cartclash.lol and www.cartclash.lol. Each address saves its own analytics choice.

Game data: Your browser stores your cart name, player ID, settings, progress, and analytics choice. Multiplayer shares your name, connection data, and game actions with the service and other players. Direct connections can expose network addresses.

Analytics: Cart Clash and Glitch collect visit and game events, performance, player/install/session IDs, and device details. Cart Clash also records approximate country and region. IDs can link visits. Cloudflare measures visits and page performance, hosts the game, and protects the service.

Logs: Requests include IP and browser information. Error reports include technical details, page addresses, times, and game versions. Diagnostic captures can contain game and connection data. Support receives your email and message.

Retention: New records replace the oldest above 20,000 game events or 2,000 error reports; there is no fixed day limit. Providers and support email have separate retention periods. Clearing browser data removes progress and the analytics choice, which resets to on.

Your requests: Contact support about access, correction, deletion, or a child's data. We may need information to locate your records. Rights depend on your location. Providers may process data abroad. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
