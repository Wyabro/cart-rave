CART CLASH

Privacy & analytics

Redshift Studios · Updated September 13, 2026

This notice covers Cart Clash on cartclash.lol and www.cartclash.lol. Redshift Studios publishes the game. Contact us at redshiftstudiossupport@gmail.com for privacy questions or support.

Your analytics choice

Optional analytics is on by default. This choice controls Cart Clash gameplay analytics, Glitch analytics, and Cloudflare Web Analytics loaded by the game.

Enable JavaScript to view and save your choice.

The choice applies to this browser and site address. Set it separately if you use another browser or switch between www.cartclash.lol and cartclash.lol. Reload other open game tabs after changing it.

Return to Cart Clash

You can also play with analytics off for this visit. Turning analytics off does not delete data already sent.

Data used to run the game

Your browser stores your cart name, random player identifier, settings, progress, and analytics choice. Multiplayer sends your cart name, connection information, and game actions to the game service and other players. Direct player connections can expose network addresses to other participants.

Cloudflare hosts and protects the service. Network requests include information such as your IP address and browser details. We use connection data to operate rooms and limit abuse.

Optional analytics

Cart Clash records sessions, matches, results, selected arenas, unlocks, exits, errors, and performance measures. Records can include random player and session identifiers, game version, site address, device details, and approximate country and region. These identifiers are not your real name, but can link activity across visits.

Glitch receives visit and game events, install and session identifiers, and device information. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits and page performance. Providers also receive the network information needed to handle requests.

Errors and support

Error reporting and security logs continue when optional analytics is off. Error reports can include the error, technical context, browser details, page address, time, and game version. If you send a diagnostic capture, it can contain more game and connection details. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send.

Storage and retention

Browser data stays until you clear it or the game replaces it. Clearing site data also removes saved progress and your analytics choice. Optional analytics returns to its default on setting after the choice is cleared.

The game analytics store keeps up to 20,000 events. The error store keeps up to 2,000 reports. New records replace the oldest when these limits are reached. These limits are based on record counts, not a fixed number of days. Service-provider records and support email have separate retention periods.

Providers and your requests

We use Cloudflare for hosting, security, and analytics, and Glitch for game analytics. Provider processing can take place outside your country. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Contact us for questions about provider handling, or to request access, correction, or deletion of data we can identify as yours. Applicable privacy rights depend on where you live.

We may need information to locate your records and confirm your request. Do not send passwords. Contact us if you are a parent or guardian with a question about a child's data.
